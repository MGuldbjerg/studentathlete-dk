CHARLOTTE, N.C. (theACC.com) – Week 13 had a group of first-time conference weekly award winners for this men’s tennis season, as Miami’s Martin Katz was tabbed the ACC Men’s Tennis Player of the Week, Stanford’s duo of Nico Godsick and Hudson Rivera was named the Doubles Team of the Week and Virginia’s Keegan Rice was voted the Freshman of the Week for matches played March 31 through April 6.
 
ACC Men’s Tennis Player of the Week
Martin Katz, Sr., Miami, Chaumont-Gistoux, Belgium
Katz led the Hurricanes to a pair of ACC wins last week, going a combined 4-0 over doubles and singles play, while also earning his second-highest career singles win.
 
In Miami’s 4-0 win over Louisville on Thursday, April 3, the senior teamed up with Antonio Prat for a 7-5 win on court one in doubles play to clinch the doubles point and then followed with a 6-1, 6-2 straight-set win on court one in singles action over Natan Rodrigues of the Cardinals.
 
In the final home match of his Canes’ career, the Belgium native helped lead the Canes to a 4-2 win over No. 55 Notre Dame with a win in both doubles and singles play. Katz and Prat cruised to a 6-1 win over No. 68 Sebastian Dominko and Chase Thomson at the number one doubles spot. He was then dominant in a 6-4, 6-0 triumph over No. 18 Dominko in singles competition.
 
ACC Men’s Tennis Doubles Team of the Week
Nico Godsick, So., Stanford, Hunting Valley, Ohio
Hudson Rivera, So., Stanford, Rancho Santa Fe, Calif.
Teaming together for just the fifth time all year, the sophomore tandem posted a pair of wins in doubles play to help lead the No. 9 Cardinal to victories over No. 47 North Carolina and No. 15 Duke last weekend.
 
In the 4-0 sweep of the Tar Heels, Godsick and Rivera clinched the doubles point with a 7-5 win over Diego Jarry and Anthony Wright of North Carolina on court three.
 
Two days later, in the 4-0 sweep of Duke, the tandem moved up to court one and recorded a 6-4 victory over the Blue Devils’ Pedro Rodenas and Andreja Petrovic, again clinching the doubles point with the win.
 
With the two wins last weekend, the duo improved to 5-0 on the season.
 
ACC Men’s Tennis Freshman of the Week
Keegan Rice, Fr., Virginia, Regina, Canada
Rice picked up his second conference weekly honor of the season by earning the first ranked singles win of his career last week by defeating No. 113 Nate Bonetto of Georgia Tech 6-2, 6-3 on court two in Virginia's 4-0 sweep at home over No. 46 Georgia Tech on Friday. In the win over the Yellow Jackets, Rice also recorded a top-15 win in doubles play, topping No. 15 Bonetto and Gianluca Carlini by a score of 6-3 with partner James Hopper.
