BRIDGEPORT, W.Va. — The Mountain East Conference announced Monday the winners of this week’s Men’s Soccer Player of the Week awards. The MEC Player of the Week awards are presented by The Health Plan.

Concord swept the MEC Men’s Soccer weekly honors, with Malher Santi Possu being named Offensive Player of the Week and Adam Leibowitz taking home Defensive Player of the Week recognition.

Possu made a difference in a pair of wins for the Mountain Lions, notching an assist in a 2-0 win at Point Park and a hat trick with an assist in a 5-0 win against West Virginia Wesleyan. The senior from Colombia scored in the 12th, 34th, and 68th minute for his second three-goal effort of the season. He leads the MEC in goals scored with 10.

Leibowitz scored a goal in each of Concord’s victories — his first two of the campaign — while helping the Mountain Lions’ back line to a pair of clean sheets. The freshman from Winter Gardens, Florida, scored in the 81st minute to seal the win against Point Park, and then capped the scoring against West Virginia Wesleyan.
