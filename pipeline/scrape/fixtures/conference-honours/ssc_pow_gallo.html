Rigg, Roane Earn SSC Women’s Soccer Player of the Week Honors

09.15.26

ORLANDO, Fla. – Lynn's Alex Rigg earned Sunshine State Conference Women's Soccer Offensive Player of the Week honors, while Embry-Riddle's Stella Roane was selected as Defensive Player of the Week for matches played during the week ending September 13.

SSC OFFENSIVE PLAYER OF THE WEEK
Alex Rigg, Fr., F, Lynn
Leeds, England
  • Helped lead Lynn to a 2-0 week with three goals, one assist and seven points across two matches.
  • Scored a brace in a 5-0 victory over Southeastern (Fla.), recording five shots and three shots on goal in 72 minutes.
  • Added a goal and an assist in a 3-0 win over Edward Waters, putting three of her four shots on target in 60 minutes.
  • Finished the week with three goals, one assist, seven points, nine shots and six shots on goal in 132 minutes.
Other Nominees: Olivia Bradley, Barry; Maja Wee, Nova Southeastern; Chloe Milson, Rollins.

SSC DEFENSIVE PLAYER OF THE WEEK
Stella Roane, Sr., GK, Embry-Riddle
Broomfield, Colo.
  • Recorded two shutouts as Embry-Riddle played to 0-0 draws at Florida Southern and Eckerd.
  • Made nine saves across the two matches, including a season-high six saves at Eckerd.
  • Became the first goalkeeper to keep Florida Southern scoreless this season, making three saves in the road draw.
  • Faced 23 shots and posted a 1.000 save percentage while recording two shutouts.
Other Nominees: Alexa-Renee Garcia, Barry; Jasmine Gallo, Eckerd; Carly Brodsky, Florida Southern.
