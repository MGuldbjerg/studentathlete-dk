Brualokken, Winn Named SSC Women's Soccer Players of the Week

09.29.26

ORLANDO, Fla. – Florida Tech’s Iselin Brualokken has been selected as the Sunshine State Conference Women’s Soccer Offensive Player of the Week, while Eckerd’s Madi Winn earned Defensive Player of the Week honors for matches played during the week ending September 27.

SSC OFFENSIVE PLAYER OF THE WEEK
Iselin Brualokken, Florida Tech
Fr. | M | Heggedal, Norway
  • Totaled five points during the week with two goals and one assist.
  • In the Panthers’ win over Lynn, scored one goal and assisted on another while recording three shots.
  • She currently ranks tied for 10th in the SSC with three goals this season and tied for fourth with four assists.
Other Nominees: Freija Aflenzer, Eckerd; Calli Berrang, Embry-Riddle; Jordan Lavery, Palm Beach Atlantic.

SSC DEFENSIVE PLAYER OF THE WEEK
Madi Winn, Eckerd
Sr. | GK | Fort Myers, Fla.
  • Played all 180 minutes in goal as Eckerd went unbeaten with a 1-0-1 record against Tampa and No. 1 Florida Tech, allowing just one goal across the two matches.
  • Winn finished the week with four saves, one shutout, and a 0.50 goals-against average.
Other Nominees: Stella Roane, Embry-Riddle.

About the SSC
Founded in 1975, the Sunshine State Conference (SSC) is headquartered in Orlando, Florida, and sponsors 21 NCAA Division II sports. SSC member institutions have won 144 NCAA national championships across 15 sports and boast a Division II-leading 89% Academic Success Rate. In 2025-26, the SSC set an NCAA Division II record with nine national team championships.
