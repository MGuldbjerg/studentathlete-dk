Emily Barrett and Jemimah Choi became Tiffin University's first-ever ITA women's tennis All Americans, as the ITA announced the 2025 honorees.

Barrett and Choi were selected as All American doubles players, as the top-ranked doubles team in the Midwest Region finished the year with a 16-4 record.

The duo also become just the third tennis players to earn All American status for Tiffin, joining men's tennis standout Ruben Fonga. Fonga was an honoree during the 2023 and 2024 seasons.

All-American recognition is awarded to doubles teams who finish in the top 10 of the final ITA National Doubles Rankings. Barrett and Choi were ranked 10th nationally.
