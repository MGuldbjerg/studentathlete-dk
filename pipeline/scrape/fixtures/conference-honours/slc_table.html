FRISCO, Texas – Bryant's Daniel Baquero and Northwestern State's Sofi Garcia have won this week's Jersey Mike's Southland Conference Tennis Players of the Week awards, as announced by the Conference Office on Tuesday. 

Men's Player of the Week: Daniel Baquero, Bryant – So. – Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic (Conexus)
Baquero was a top performer for Bryant in a hard fought battle against NJIT last weelk. He defeated the Highlanders' Sorin in No. 6 singles: 6-3, 6-3 and also won his doubles match alongside teammate Myles Peterson: 7-5.

Women's Player of the Week: Sofi Garcia, Northwestern State –  Jr. – Buenos Aires, Argentina (SEADEA)
Garcia has been instrumental in the Northwestern State Lady Demons' recent successes, delivering standout performances in her last two matches. On March 10 against Texas A&M-Corpus Christi, Garcia dominated at No. 6 singles, defeating Juliette Jalai 6-0, 6-3, contributing significantly to the team's 4-3 victory, and keeping them undefeated in Southland Conference play. On Friday, March 7, facing UTRGV, she secured a hard-fought win over Mariia Bakhtina, 7-5, 7-6 (7-3), clinching the match for the Lady Demons.
